# Space Invaders

A browser-based Space Invaders arcade game built with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript, featuring **Easy**, **Normal**, and **Hard** difficulty modes.

## Difficulty Modes
* **Easy (`E`):** 3 rows of aliens, slower movement, lower enemy fire rate, 15 damage per hit (75 pts/kill).
* **Normal (`N`):** 4 rows of aliens, balanced speed and fire rate, 20 damage per hit (100 pts/kill).
* **Hard (`H`):** 5 rows of aliens, fast movement, heavy enemy fire, 25 damage per hit (150 pts/kill).

## Controls
* **Select Difficulty:** Click a difficulty button on the start screen or press `E`, `N`, or `H`
* **Move Left / Right:** `A` / `D` or `Left` / `Right` Arrow Keys
* **Shoot:** `Spacebar`
* **Switch Weapon Modes (uses Energy):**
  * `1` — **Standard** (Single laser, 0 energy cost)
  * `2` — **Rapid** (Dual lasers, 15 energy to activate + 2 per shot)
  * `3` — **Spread** (Triple angled lasers, 25 energy to activate + 5 per shot)
* **Pause / Resume:** `P`
* **Return to Menu:** `R` (on Win or Game Over screen)
